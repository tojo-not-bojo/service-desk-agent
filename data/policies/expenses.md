# Expenses Policy

## Submitting a claim
Submit expense claims through the finance portal within 30 days of spending the money. Claims submitted more than 90 days later will not be paid.

## Receipts
Receipts are required for all claims over £10. Photos of receipts are accepted.

## Travel
Book rail travel in standard class. Mileage for using your own car is paid at 45p per mile. Taxis are only allowed when no reasonable public transport is available, or between 22:00 and 06:00.

## Hotels and meals
The nightly hotel limit is £120, or £160 in London. Meals while travelling overnight are covered up to £35 per day. Alcohol cannot be claimed.
