# IT Equipment and Acceptable Use Policy

## Your equipment
Company laptops are replaced every 4 years. Do not install unapproved software. Request new software through the IT service desk.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours, by phone if possible. The device will be locked and wiped remotely.

## Passwords and accounts
Never share your password, including with IT staff. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access.

## Personal use
Limited personal use of company equipment is allowed outside working hours, provided it doesn't break any other policy.
