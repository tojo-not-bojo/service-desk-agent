# Hybrid Working Policy

## Office attendance
Staff in hybrid roles are expected to work from the office at least 2 days per week. Teams may agree specific anchor days.

## Working from home
You must have a suitable, private workspace and a reliable internet connection. Company laptops must be used for all work. Personal devices must not be used to access company systems.

## Working abroad
Working from outside the UK is allowed for up to 10 working days per year, with line manager and HR approval requested at least 4 weeks in advance. Some countries are excluded for security reasons. HR holds the current list.

## Equipment and costs
The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset. A one-off home office allowance of £150 can be claimed through expenses within your first 3 months.
