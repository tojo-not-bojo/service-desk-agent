# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus UK bank holidays. Part-time staff receive a pro-rata amount based on their contracted hours. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Booking leave
Book leave through the HR portal. Requests for more than 3 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Your line manager approves requests and should respond within 3 working days.

## Carrying over leave
You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost. Exceptions for long-term sickness or parental leave are handled by HR.

## Buying and selling leave
Once a year, in November, you can apply to buy up to 5 extra days of leave. Selling leave is not permitted.
